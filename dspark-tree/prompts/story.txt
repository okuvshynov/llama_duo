The lighthouse keeper found the boat on a Tuesday, which was wrong in itself, because nothing ever happened on Tuesdays. It had come ashore in the night, quiet as a secret, and now it sat wedged between the black rocks below the north cliff with its sail neatly furled and its oars shipped, as though someone had parked it there and gone off to run an errand.
